PAGE 03 · OPEN WHEN YOU NEED A SMILE

A letter for your birthday

Dear Sweet Cherry,

Happy Birthday! Today is all about celebrating you and the lovely person you are. I hope this little journal makes your day feel a bit more magical, like opening a tiny box full of happy things.

I hope the year ahead brings you gentle mornings, exciting adventures, kind people, and dreams that slowly turn into beautiful memories. May you always find reasons to laugh, moments that make you feel proud, and little surprises when you least expect them.

On days when life feels busy or a little difficult, remember to be kind to yourself. You deserve the same care and warmth that you give to others. Keep your hopes close, keep trying the things that make your heart happy, and never forget that being yourself is something worth celebrating.

So here’s to more cake, more cuddles, more silly moments, more adventures, and a year filled with things that make you smile. I hope this birthday is only the beginning of something wonderful.

With lots of birthday love,
Your little journal 💗
